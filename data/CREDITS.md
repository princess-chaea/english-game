# Vocabulary data credits

## 2022 revised English curriculum

The word list, recommendation markers, and curriculum vocabulary limits are based on the Korean Ministry of Education notice No. 2022-33, Appendix 14, English Curriculum (`[별책14] 영어과 교육과정`).

The app's individual grade and difficulty placements are derived educational metadata, not official Ministry word-by-grade labels.

## Korean meaning drafts

Draft Korean meanings, IPA, CEFR, frequency, and part-of-speech metadata are derived in part from [jhseo1211/open-english-korean-dict](https://github.com/jhseo1211/open-english-korean-dict), licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Accordingly, the derived meaning/metadata portions of `curriculum-3000-with-meanings.json`, `word-packs.json`, and `word-packs.js` are made available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Entries tagged `meaningStatus: "draft"` should be reviewed before being treated as authoritative Korean definitions.
